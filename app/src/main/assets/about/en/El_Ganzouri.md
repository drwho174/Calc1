# El-Ganzouri score

Preoperative assessment of the difficult intubation risk by seven criteria
available at the bedside. The total score ranges from 0 to 11.

### Criteria

| Criterion | 0 points | 1 point | 2 points |
|---|---|---|---|
| Mallampati class | I–II | III | IV |
| Thyromental distance | ≥ 6.5 cm | 6–6.4 cm | < 6 cm |
| Mouth opening | ≥ 5 cm | < 5 cm | — |
| Neck mobility | > 90° | 80–90° | < 80° |
| Mandibular protrusion | possible | impossible | — |
| Body weight | < 110 kg | 110–120 kg | > 120 kg |
| Difficult intubation in the history | no | yes | — |

### Interpretation

| Total score | Risk class |
|---|---|
| 0–2 | Low |
| 3–4 | Moderate |
| ≥ 5 | High |

At a high risk class alternative airway management techniques and the presence
of an experienced anaesthetist are planned in advance.
