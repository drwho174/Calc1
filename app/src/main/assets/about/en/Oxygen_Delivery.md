# Oxygen delivery

This calculator estimates the indexed ratio of oxygen delivery to carbon dioxide production during cardiopulmonary bypass.

Values of $$ DO2_i / VCO2_i < 5 $$ are predictors of hyperlactataemia (a lactate concentration above 3 mmol/L).

Original article: [Anaerobic Metabolism During Cardiopulmonary Bypass: Predictive Value of Carbon Dioxide Derived Parameters](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.athoracsur.2006.01.025)
