# Lee index (Revised Cardiac Risk Index, RCRI)

Estimates the risk of major cardiac complications (MACE: cardiac death,
myocardial infarction, cardiac arrest) in noncardiac surgery.
Six independent predictors, total 0–6.

### Criteria

| Predictor | Points |
|---|---|
| High-risk surgery (intrathoracic, intra-abdominal, vascular above the inguinal ligament) | 1 |
| Ischaemic heart disease | 1 |
| Heart failure | 1 |
| Stroke or TIA in the history | 1 |
| Diabetes requiring insulin | 1 |
| Creatinine > 177 µmol/L (2 mg/dL) | 1 |

### Interpretation

| Score | MACE risk |
|---|---|
| 0 | 0.4 % |
| 1 | 0.9 % |
| 2 | 7 % |
| ≥ 3 | 11 % |

The values correspond to the validation cohort of the original study.
At a score of 2 and higher, additional cardiac evaluation and perioperative
monitoring are discussed.
