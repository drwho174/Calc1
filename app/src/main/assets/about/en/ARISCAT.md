# ARISCAT

The index predicts the risk of postoperative pulmonary complications (pneumonia,
respiratory failure, atelectasis, bronchospasm, pleural effusion, pneumothorax,
aspiration pneumonitis) after noncardiac surgery.

### Criteria

| Factor | Points |
|---|---|
| Age | ≤ 50 years — 0; 51–80 years — 3; > 80 years — 16 |
| Preoperative SpO2 | ≥ 96 % — 0; 91–95 % — 8; ≤ 90 % — 24 |
| Respiratory infection in the last month | 17 |
| Anaemia (Hb ≤ 10 g/dL) | 11 |
| Incision | peripheral — 0; upper laparotomy — 15; thoracotomy — 24 |
| Surgery duration | < 2 h — 0; 2–3 h — 16; > 3 h — 23 |
| Emergency surgery | 8 |

### Interpretation

| Total score | Risk |
|---|---|
| < 26 | Low |
| 26–44 | Moderate |
| ≥ 45 | High |

At moderate and high risk, perioperative respiratory support, early
mobilisation, breathing exercises and avoidance of prolonged immobilisation are
discussed.
