# Haemoglobin on cardiopulmonary bypass

This calculator is used to estimate the expected haemoglobin during cardiopulmonary bypass.
It helps to choose a transfusion strategy.

The calculator uses the following blood volume formulas:
1. Allen for men $$ EBV = 0.000417 * H^{3} + 45 * W - 30 $$
2. Allen for women $$ EBV = 0.000414 * H^{3} + 45 * W - 30 $$

<br> Where EBV is estimated blood volume; H is height in centimetres; W is weight in kilograms

1. Nadler for men $$ EBV = 367 * H^{3} + 32.2 * W + 604 $$
2. Nadler for women $$ EBV = 356 * H^{3} + 33.1 * W + 183 $$

<br> Where EBV is estimated blood volume; H is height in metres; W is weight in kilograms
