# Charlson Comorbidity Index

The index predicts 10-year survival taking comorbidities and age into account.
Each condition weighs 1, 2, 3 or 6 points; 1 point is added for every decade
after the age of 50.

### Points

| Weight | Conditions |
|-----|-----------|
| **1** | Myocardial infarction, heart failure, peripheral arterial disease, stroke/TIA, dementia, chronic lung disease, connective tissue disease, peptic ulcer disease, liver disease without portal hypertension, diabetes without end-organ damage |
| **2** | Hemiplegia, moderate or severe kidney disease, diabetes with end-organ damage, solid tumour, leukaemia, lymphoma |
| **3** | Liver disease with portal hypertension |
| **6** | Metastatic tumour, AIDS |

### Age

| Age | Points |
|---------|-------|
| < 50 years | 0 |
| 50–59 years | +1 |
| 60–69 years | +2 |
| 70–79 years | +3 |
| ≥ 80 years | +4 |

### Interpretation

| Index | 10-year survival |
|--------|------------------------|
| 0 | 99 % |
| 1 | 96 % |
| 2 | 90 % |
| 3 | 77 % |
| 4 | 53 % |
| 5 | 21 % |
| ≥ 6 | 2 % |
